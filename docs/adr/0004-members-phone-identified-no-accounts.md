# Members are identified by phone number, with no account

A Member has no login and no password; their phone number is their identity, and they reach KORU
via a link or QR code. This minimises friction for givers — many of whom will not install or
register for anything — at the cost of weaker identity guarantees. Staff, by contrast, are Users
with real logins. Anonymous Payments (no Member attached at all) are also permitted.
